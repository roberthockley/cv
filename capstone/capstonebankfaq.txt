What are your opening hours?
Weekdays: AlpenBank branches are open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 5 pm.
Saturdays: The Orchard, and Raffles Place branches are open Saturdays from 10 am to 2 pm.


Are you open on Sundays?
AlpenBank branches are closed on Sundays.


Do all branches offer account opening services?
Most AlpenBank branches offer account opening services. Please check your specific branch for details.


Which branches offer loans?
Loans are available at selected branches including City Hall, and Raffles Place.


Which branches offer cash withdrawal and deposit services?
Cash withdrawal and deposit services are available at selected branches such as Orchard and City Hall.


What is wealth consultation?
Our wealth consultation service connects you with a financial specialist who can advise on investment strategies, retirement planning, and portfolio diversification tailored to your goals.


What investment services do you offer?
We offer a range of investment options including mutual funds, bonds, structured products, and managed portfolios. Availability may vary by branch.


What is business banking?
Business banking provides financial services for companies, including corporate accounts, trade financing, payroll solutions, and business loans.


Do you offer remittance services?
Yes. Selected branches offer international remittance services with competitive exchange rates and secure transfers.


What support do you provide for card issues?
Our card support service can help with lost or stolen cards, PIN resets, transaction disputes, and card activation.


What is fraud support?
Fraud support assists customers who suspect unauthorised activity. Our team can freeze accounts, investigate transactions, and guide you through next steps.


Can I open a bank account online?
For this demo environment, account opening is handled in-branch. Please visit a participating branch with valid identification.


Do you provide financial advice for first-time investors?
Yes. Our advisors can help first-time investors understand risk levels, diversification, and long-term planning strategies.


How long does it take to process a loan application?
For demo purposes, most loan applications are reviewed within 3–5 business days, depending on documentation completeness.


Is there a minimum balance requirement?
Minimum balance requirements vary by account type. Please speak to a branch advisor for personalised guidance.
